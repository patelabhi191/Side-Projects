# Favour for U

### *Ask once. Check in daily.*

A tiny, two-person accountability app — built for my aunt, as a favour.



## The story

My aunt kept asking whether the homework got done. Every evening. Same question, same forgetting, same low-grade friction between two people who love each other.

So I built her this. One person sets a favour — *"Did you finish your homework?"* — picks a time and a grace window. The other person taps **Mark done**. If nobody checks in before the grace window closes, it slides into **Missed**, and my aunt knows without having to ask again.

That's the whole app. It doesn't do projects, sprints, tags, or priorities. It answers one question — **did the thing get done today?** — and gets out of the way.

> Named \\\*Favour for U\\\* because it's a favour app, and because it was a favour. 💜
